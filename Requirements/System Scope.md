# Purpose

This document defines the scope of the AI-Powered Rural Root Cause Discovery & Evidence-Based Decision Support System.

It establishes the boundaries of the project by identifying what is included, what is excluded, and the intended operational environment.

A clearly defined scope helps prevent misunderstandings, uncontrolled feature expansion, and inconsistent implementation.

---

# Scope Statement

The project aims to develop an intelligent decision support platform that assists rural governance by collecting survey data, validating evidence, identifying recurring issues, discovering probable root causes, and providing explainable recommendations to authorized decision-makers.

The platform supports administrative decision-making through evidence-based analysis while ensuring transparency and human oversight.

---

# In Scope

The project includes the following capabilities:

## Data Collection

- Capture structured rural survey data.
- Record citizen complaints.
- Store geographic (GPS) information.
- Upload and manage supporting images.
- Maintain centralized survey records.

---

## Data Management

- Secure storage of survey information.
- Complaint management.
- Evidence organization.
- User management.
- Data retrieval and search.

---

## AI-Based Analysis

The system will support:

- Complaint similarity analysis.
- Identification of recurring issues.
- Root cause discovery.
- Evidence aggregation.
- Explainable recommendation generation.
- Confidence reporting for AI outputs where applicable.

---

## Decision Support

The platform will provide:

- Administrative dashboards.
- Issue prioritization.
- Analytical summaries.
- Evidence visualization.
- Recommendation reports.

---

## Security

The project includes:

- User authentication.
- Role-based access control.
- Secure data storage.
- Audit logging.
- Data integrity protection.

---

## Reporting

Generate reports including:

- Survey summaries.
- District-level issue analysis.
- Root cause reports.
- Recommendation summaries.
- Evidence-backed decision reports.

---

# Out of Scope

The following are intentionally excluded from the current project scope:

- Autonomous government decision-making.
- Automatic approval of public policies.
- Financial management systems.
- Citizen payment processing.
- Emergency response systems.
- Predictive analytics without supporting evidence.
- Real-time IoT sensor integration.
- Drone-based field monitoring.
- Satellite imagery analysis.
- Integration with external government databases unless explicitly defined in future phases.

---

# Intended Users

The system is designed for:

- Field Officers
- Government Officials
- District Administrators
- System Administrators
- Technical Maintenance Team

Citizens participate indirectly by contributing survey information and community issue reports.

---

# Geographic Scope

The platform is intended for rural administrative environments.

Although initially designed for pilot deployments, the architecture should support future expansion to:

- Multiple villages
- Mandals
- Districts
- State-wide deployments

without requiring major architectural changes.

---

# Operational Scope

The system supports:

- Survey management
- Complaint analysis
- AI-assisted decision support
- Administrative reporting
- Evidence management

The platform assists decision-makers but does not replace human judgment.

# Scope Constraints

The project operates under the following constraints:

- AI recommendations require supporting evidence.
- Final decisions remain with authorized officials.
- System behavior follows documented business rules.
- Data privacy and security must be maintained.
- Requirements outside this document require formal approval before implementation.

---

# Success Criteria

The project scope is considered successfully implemented when:

- All in-scope features are operational.
- No excluded functionality is implemented unintentionally.
- Stakeholders can perform their intended workflows.
- AI recommendations remain evidence-based and explainable.
- The system satisfies the functional and non-functional requirements.

# Scope Statement

> The AI-Powered Rural Root Cause Discovery & Evidence-Based Decision Support System provides an evidence-driven platform for collecting, analyzing, and interpreting rural survey information to support transparent and informed administrative decision-making. The project focuses on intelligent decision support while maintaining clear system boundaries, human oversight, and explainable AI.

# Guiding Principle

A successful project delivers everything within its defined scope—and avoids building what was never required.

Every proposed feature should be evaluated against this document before being approved for development.
