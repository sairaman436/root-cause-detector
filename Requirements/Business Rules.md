# Purpose

This document defines the business rules governing the operation of the AI-Powered Rural Root Cause Discovery & Evidence-Based Decision Support System.

Business rules represent organizational policies and operational constraints that the software must consistently enforce to maintain accuracy, transparency, and integrity.

---

# Scope

These rules apply to:

- Survey collection
- Complaint management
- Evidence handling
- AI analysis
- Decision support
- User management
- Security
- Administrative operations

---

# Rule Categories

The business rules are grouped into:

- User Management
- Survey Management
- Evidence Management
- Complaint Management
- AI Decision Support
- Administrative Operations
- Security & Audit

---

# User Management Rules

### BR-001

Every user shall possess a unique authenticated account.

---

### BR-002

User permissions shall be determined by assigned roles.

---

### BR-003

Unauthorized users shall not access protected resources.

---

# Survey Management Rules

### BR-004

Every survey record shall be associated with the field officer who created it.

---

### BR-005

Mandatory survey fields shall be completed before submission.

---

### BR-006

Once a survey is submitted, any modifications shall be logged for audit purposes.

---

### BR-007

Each survey shall receive a unique identifier.

---

# Evidence Management Rules

### BR-008

Supporting images shall remain linked to their corresponding survey records.

---

### BR-009

GPS information shall be associated with the correct survey whenever location data is available.

---

### BR-010

Evidence shall not be removed without appropriate authorization.

---

# Complaint Management Rules

### BR-011

Each complaint shall be linked to an existing survey or authorized record.

---

### BR-012

Duplicate complaints may be grouped for analytical purposes but shall remain individually traceable.

---

### BR-013

Complaint history shall be preserved for future analysis.

---

# AI Decision Support Rules

### BR-014

AI recommendations shall only be generated when sufficient supporting evidence exists.

---

### BR-015

Every AI recommendation shall provide an explanation of the evidence used.

---

### BR-016

AI recommendations shall assist human decision-makers and shall not automatically trigger administrative actions.

---

### BR-017

Confidence information shall accompany AI recommendations whenever supported by the underlying model.

---

# Administrative Rules

### BR-018

Authorized administrators shall manage user accounts and system configuration.

---

### BR-019

Administrative actions affecting critical data shall be recorded in audit logs.

---

### BR-020

Reports generated by the system shall reflect the latest approved data.

---

# Security Rules

### BR-021

User authentication shall be required before accessing protected functionality.

---

### BR-022

Role-based access control shall be enforced consistently across all modules.

---

### BR-023

Sensitive information shall only be accessible to authorized personnel.

---

### BR-024

Audit logs shall record significant security-related events.

---

# Data Integrity Rules

### BR-025

Each survey record shall maintain referential integrity with related complaints, evidence, and analytical results.

---

### BR-026

System-generated identifiers shall remain unique throughout the project lifecycle.

---

### BR-027

Deleted records shall follow the project's retention and audit policies where applicable.

---

# Exception Handling Rules

### BR-028

Incomplete surveys shall not proceed to AI analysis.

---

### BR-029

Missing evidence shall not prevent data storage but may reduce the confidence of AI-generated recommendations.

---

### BR-030

System failures shall not result in silent data loss.

# Success Criteria

Business rules are considered successfully implemented when:

- System behavior consistently follows documented organizational policies.
- Unauthorized actions are prevented.
- AI recommendations remain evidence-based and explainable.
- Critical operations are fully traceable through audit logs.
- Data integrity is preserved throughout the system lifecycle.

# Guiding Principle

Business rules ensure that the system behaves consistently, transparently, and in accordance with organizational policies.

Every implemented feature should respect the business rules defined in this document.
