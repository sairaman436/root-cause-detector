# Purpose

This document identifies all stakeholders involved in the project, their roles, responsibilities, expectations, and interactions with the system.

Understanding stakeholder needs ensures that system requirements align with real-world operational and administrative objectives.

---

# Stakeholder Overview

The project involves multiple stakeholder groups, each interacting with the system in different ways.

The stakeholders include:

- Citizens
- Field Officers
- Government Officials
- District Administrators
- System Administrators
- AI & Technical Team

---

# Stakeholder Classification

| Stakeholder | Category | Role |
|-------------|----------|------|
| Citizens | External | Report community issues and participate in surveys |
| Field Officers | Operational | Collect survey data and supporting evidence |
| Government Officials | Decision Makers | Review insights and make administrative decisions |
| District Administrators | Management | Monitor regional performance and resource allocation |
| System Administrators | Technical | Maintain the platform and manage users |
| AI & Development Team | Technical | Build, maintain, and improve the system |

---

# Stakeholder Details

## 1. Citizens

### Responsibilities

- Participate in surveys.
- Provide accurate information.
- Report local issues.

### Expectations

- Faster resolution of community problems.
- Transparent governance.
- Fair prioritization of issues.

### System Interaction

Citizens contribute data indirectly through surveys and issue reporting.

---

## 2. Field Officers

### Responsibilities

- Conduct field surveys.
- Capture GPS coordinates.
- Upload photographs.
- Verify collected information.

### Expectations

- Easy-to-use data collection interface.
- Reliable survey submission.
- Reduced manual paperwork.

### System Interaction

Field officers act as the primary data collection layer of the system.

---

## 3. Government Officials

### Responsibilities

- Analyze reports.
- Review AI recommendations.
- Prioritize development activities.
- Make final administrative decisions.

### Expectations

- Accurate insights.
- Explainable recommendations.
- Reliable dashboards.
- Faster issue identification.

### System Interaction

Government officials primarily consume analytical reports and decision support outputs.

---

## 4. District Administrators

### Responsibilities

- Monitor district-wide performance.
- Allocate resources.
- Review recurring rural issues.
- Track intervention outcomes.

### Expectations

- District-level analytics.
- Trend visualization.
- Performance reports.
- Evidence-backed recommendations.

### System Interaction

District administrators use strategic dashboards and analytical summaries.

---

## 5. System Administrators

### Responsibilities

- Manage user accounts.
- Configure system settings.
- Maintain security.
- Monitor system availability.
- Manage backups.

### Expectations

- Stable infrastructure.
- Secure access controls.
- Efficient administration tools.

### System Interaction

System administrators oversee platform operations without participating in governance decisions.

---

## 6. AI & Development Team

### Responsibilities

- Develop system components.
- Train and evaluate AI models.
- Maintain software quality.
- Improve system performance.
- Ensure compliance with engineering standards.

### Expectations

- Clear requirements.
- Reliable datasets.
- Maintainable architecture.
- Comprehensive documentation.

### System Interaction

The technical team maintains and evolves the platform throughout its lifecycle.

# Stakeholder Needs

The system should enable stakeholders to:

- Collect reliable field data.
- Access verified information.
- Analyze recurring community issues.
- Receive explainable recommendations.
- Make informed decisions using evidence.
- Monitor system performance and governance outcomes.

---

# Stakeholder Success Criteria

Stakeholder expectations are considered satisfied when:

- Survey collection is efficient.
- Data quality is maintained.
- Decision-makers receive timely insights.
- AI recommendations remain transparent and explainable.
- Administrative actions are supported by verifiable evidence.

---

# Stakeholder Statement

> The success of this project depends on delivering meaningful value to every stakeholder, from citizens contributing field information to government officials making evidence-based decisions.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every requirement in this project should satisfy the needs of one or more identified stakeholders.

Stakeholder value remains the primary measure of project success.
