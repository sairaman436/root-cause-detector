# Incident Review Template

## Purpose

Use this template after production, security, data, AI, or operational incidents.

## Required Content

### Summary

Describe what happened, who was affected, severity, and duration.

### Timeline

Record detection, declaration, mitigation, recovery, and closure times.

### Impact

Describe user impact, data impact, AI decision impact, financial impact, and compliance impact.

### Root Cause

Identify technical, process, monitoring, and organizational contributors.

### Detection And Response

Evaluate alerts, dashboards, logs, runbooks, and communication.

### Corrective Actions

List owners, deadlines, tests, documentation, and policy changes.

### Lessons

Record durable lessons for [Project Memory](../memory/PROJECT_MEMORY.md).

## Anti-Patterns

- Blaming individuals.
- Closing without preventive actions.
- Ignoring near misses.
- Failing to update runbooks or tests.
