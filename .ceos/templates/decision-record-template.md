# Decision Record Template

## Purpose

Use this template to record significant engineering, architecture, AI, data, security, or operations decisions.

## Required Content

### Title

Use a clear action-oriented title.

### Status

Choose one: Proposed, Accepted, Superseded, Deprecated, Rejected.

### Context

Describe the problem, constraints, affected systems, and why a decision is required now.

### Decision

State the selected decision in concrete terms.

### Alternatives Considered

List serious alternatives and why they were not selected.

### Rationale

Explain why this decision best satisfies platform principles, risk constraints, and long-term maintainability.

### Tradeoffs

State what becomes harder because of this decision.

### Consequences

Describe expected implementation, operational, security, data, and documentation consequences.

### Review And Approval

List required boards from [Review Boards](../organization/review-boards.md) and approval evidence.

### Related Documents

Link related constitutions, standards, proposals, issues, and implementation files.

## Anti-Patterns

- Recording only the chosen option without alternatives.
- Hiding tradeoffs.
- Using a decision record for trivial implementation details.
- Leaving status ambiguous.
