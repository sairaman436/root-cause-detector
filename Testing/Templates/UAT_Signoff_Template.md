# Purpose

This document formally records business acceptance of the solution after successful completion of User Acceptance Testing (UAT). It authorizes the project to proceed toward production deployment following stakeholder approval.

---

# UAT Objectives

The objectives of UAT are to:

- Validate business requirements
- Verify end-to-end workflows
- Confirm usability
- Validate reporting
- Verify AI recommendations
- Confirm operational readiness
- Validate business rules
- Support production approval

---

# UAT Scope

## In Scope

- Authentication
- User Management
- Survey Management
- AI Root Cause Prediction
- Recommendation Engine
- Reporting
- Notifications
- Administrative Functions
- Dashboard Analytics

---

## Out of Scope

- Future releases
- Experimental features
- Infrastructure performance benchmarking
- Third-party applications outside project scope

# Acceptance Criteria

The solution shall satisfy the following criteria before sign-off:

- All critical business workflows executed successfully.
- Business requirements validated.
- AI recommendations acceptable for business use.
- No Critical defects remain open.
- No High defects remain open unless formally accepted.
- Required reports generated successfully.
- User documentation available.
- Training completed where applicable.
