# 📅 Engineering Meeting Templates

Version: 1.0

Project: AI Rural Root Cause Discovery System

---

# Purpose

This document provides standardized templates for documenting engineering meetings.

Consistent meeting records improve transparency, accountability, decision tracking, and project governance.

Every significant meeting should produce documented outcomes and action items.

---

# Meeting Types

The project uses the following meeting categories:

- Sprint Planning
- Daily Stand-up
- Weekly Progress Review
- Architecture Review
- AI Model Review
- Risk Review
- Code Review
- Release Readiness Review
- Retrospective

# Meeting Record Guidelines

Every meeting record should include:

- Date
- Participants
- Agenda
- Decisions
- Action Items
- Responsible Owners
- Due Dates

Meeting notes should be stored in chronological order and linked to related ADRs, Decision Logs, or Risk Register entries where applicable.

---

# Final Principle

Meetings should produce decisions, not just discussions.

Every engineering meeting should leave behind a clear record of what was decided, why it was decided, who is responsible, and what happens next.
