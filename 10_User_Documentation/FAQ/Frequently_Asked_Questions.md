# Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the AI Rural Root Cause Discovery System?
**A**: It is an enterprise government analytics platform that combines rural survey data, physical water testing, and AI machine learning models to discover the underlying root causes of rural infrastructure and agricultural issues, offering transparent evidence-based decision support for policy makers.

### Q2: Can field officers use the application without internet connectivity?
**A**: Yes. The mobile survey app includes an offline SQLite mode that securely encrypts and stores survey data and photographs locally on the device until an internet connection is established.

### Q3: How does the system ensure AI predictions are accurate and unbiased?
**A**: Every AI prediction outputs TreeSHAP feature attribution charts showing exact ground evidence driving the recommendation. Furthermore, models undergo monthly algorithmic fairness audits to ensure demographic parity across all districts.

### Q4: Can AI models automatically allocate public works funds?
**A**: No. The platform operates on a **Human-in-the-Loop** model. AI models provide evidence and recommendations, but final project approval and budget allocation require explicit digital signatures from authorized Government Officials.

### Q5: How is citizen data privacy protected?
**A**: All personal identifying information (PII) is automatically scrubbed and masked before survey records enter the feature store. Data is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and at rest via AES-256 KMS encryption.
